---
title: "Team members"
description: "Add engineers and office staff, and control what they can see."
---

You can add team members to your GasPro account so everyone works from the same
customers, jobs and documents. Each member signs in with their own email and
password, on mobile or desktop.

Team members appear in **Assigned To** on jobs and in the calendar's engineer
selector on desktop.

<Note>
  Only the main account user can manage team members, and the account needs an
  active subscription. On desktop, **Manage Users** appears in the sidebar for
  the main user only.
</Note>

## Add a team member

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Mobile">
    1. Open **Settings → Manage Users**.
    2. Tap **Create User**.
    3. Enter their **Full Name**, **Email Address** and **Phone**.
    4. Add their **Gas Safe ID** and **Oil Registration ID** if they hold them.
    5. Review the **Additional Team Member** price shown on screen.
    6. Tap **Save**.
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Desktop">
    1. Open **Manage Users** in the sidebar.
    2. Select **Create User**.
    3. Enter **Full Name**, **Email**, **Phone Number**, **Gas Safe ID**,
       **Oil Registration ID** and **CSC ID** where applicable.
    4. Review the **Additional Team Member** price shown on screen.
    5. Select **Create User**.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

Adding a member adds a per-member charge to your subscription. The first payment
is prorated for the time left in your current billing cycle; future renewals are
billed at the full rate.

## Permissions

Open a team member and choose **Permissions**. The available permissions are:

| Permission | What it allows |
| --- | --- |
| View All Certificates / View All Forms | See certificates created by anyone in the company, not just their own. |
| View All Invoices | See all company invoices. |
| View All Estimates | See all company estimates. |
| View All Quotes | See all company quotes. |
| Edit All Calendar Events | Edit any calendar event, regardless of who created it. |

When a permission is off, the member only sees records they created. Turn on the
permissions they need, then select **Save Permissions** or **Save**.

## Edit or remove a member

Open the member from **Manage Users** and update their details or permissions.
To remove them, use **Delete** on their record.

<Warning>
  Removing a member stops their access, but records they created stay in the
  account. Check their open jobs and drafts before removing them.
</Warning>

## Search and manage users

On desktop, use the search box to find a member by name, email, phone or Gas
Safe ID. On mobile, scroll the **Manage Users** list and tap a member to edit
them.
