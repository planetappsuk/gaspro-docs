The calendar shows your appointments. You can link an appointment to a customer,
a job address and a job, so the right details are ready when you arrive.

## Create an appointment

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Mobile">
    1. Open **Dashboard → Calendar**.
    2. Choose **1 Day**, **3 Days**, **Week** or **Month**.
    3. Tap and drag on the calendar to create an appointment, or create one
       from the **Create** tab.
    4. Enter a **Title**. This is required.
    5. Add a **Description**, choose **All Day Appointment** if needed, and set
       the **Start Date & Time** and **End Date & Time**.
    6. Set an **Alert** to be reminded before the appointment.
    7. Optionally link a **Job** or select a **Customer** and **Job Address**.
    8. Tap **Save**.
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Desktop">
    1. Open **Calendar** in the sidebar.
    2. Select the engineer whose calendar you want to view, or leave it on
       yourself.
    3. Click a date or time slot.
    4. Enter a **Title** and optional **Description**.
    5. Choose **All Day Event** if needed, and set the **Start Date & Time** and
       **End Date & Time**.
    6. Choose **Assigned To** and optionally link a **Customer** and **Job
       Address**. You can add a new customer or address from the same dialog.
    7. Tick **Create a job sheet for this appointment?** if you want a job sheet
       created with it.
    8. Select **Save**.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Edit or delete an appointment

Open the appointment from the calendar to view or change it. On desktop you can
edit any appointment assigned to you, or any appointment if you have the **Edit
All Calendar Events** permission. On mobile the same permission controls whether
you can edit appointments created by other team members.

Use **Delete** to remove the appointment. Deleting an appointment does not
delete the linked customer, job address, job or job sheet.

## Send confirmations and reminders

Open an appointment and choose **Send Confirmation** or **Send Reminder**. Both
apps let you send by **Email** or **SMS**:

1. Check the **To** address or number.
2. For email, check the **Subject**.
3. Edit the **Message** if needed.
4. Select **Send Email** or **Send SMS**. On desktop, confirm with **Send now**.

The **Recent Messages** section shows messages already sent for the appointment.

## Create documents from an appointment

From an appointment you can also create:

- An **Invoice**, **Quote** or **Estimate**.
- A certificate.
- A **Job Sheet**, where available.

On desktop, open the appointment and use the document actions on the page. When
creating an appointment, tick **Create a job sheet for this appointment?** to
create the job sheet at the same time.

On mobile, open the appointment and use the **Actions** and document cards.

## Link a job to an appointment

Linking a job fills in the customer and job address from that job. On mobile,
open the appointment and use **Link Job**; remove the link with **Unlink Job**
if you need to choose a different customer instead.

On desktop, choose the **Customer** and **Job Address** in the appointment
dialog. If a job sheet exists for the appointment, a **View Job Sheet** button
appears.

## Calendar views

| View | Best for |
| --- | --- |
| 1 Day | A detailed day with exact times |
| 3 Days | Planning the next few days |
| Week | Seeing the week at a glance |
| Month | Long-range planning |

Desktop opens in a month view. Change the view using the calendar's view
buttons.
