The dashboard is your starting point. It shows the main areas of GasPro and,
on desktop, a live summary of recent work.

## Mobile

The **Dashboard** tab is a menu of tiles. Tap a tile to open that area.

- **Certificates** — create a certificate or view all saved certificates.
- **Jobs** — create and manage jobs.
- **Invoices, Quotes, Estimates** — create or view billing documents.
- **Calendar & Customers** — open the calendar, customers or job addresses.
- **Tools** — gas rate calculator and installation volume/tightness test.
- **Resources** — boiler manuals.
- **Import** — import customers, job addresses or appliances.
- **Support & Settings** — Help Centre, support chat, notifications,
  integrations and settings.

The bottom bar has three tabs: **Dashboard**, **Create** and **Settings**.

The **Create** tab is a shortcut for starting something new: a job, calendar
event, invoice, quote or estimate.

## Desktop

The sidebar contains:

- **Dashboard**
- **Jobs**
- **Certificates**
- **Invoices**
- **Quotes**
- **Estimates**
- **Boiler Manuals**
- **Calendar**
- **Customers**
- **Job Addresses**
- **Manage Users** (main user only)
- **Settings**

The dashboard also has quick actions for creating a certificate, invoice, quote,
estimate or job, followed by lists of your recent jobs, certificates, invoices,
quotes and estimates. Select any record to open it.

<Tip>
  On desktop you can collapse the sidebar using the handle at its edge. This
  leaves more room for tables and forms.
</Tip>

## Mobile sync

The mobile app keeps a local copy of your company data so it opens quickly on
site. You can keep working on site without signal; changes sync back to your
account when you are connected.

If something created on desktop is missing on mobile, refresh the list or use
**Re-Sync Data** where it is offered. Check your internet connection first.
