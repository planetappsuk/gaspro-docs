---
title: "Boiler manuals"
description: "Find manufacturer manuals on site, and upload your own PDF manuals."
---

GasPro gives you a library of boiler manuals by brand. Use it on site to check
installation and servicing details without carrying paper manuals.

## Find a manual

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Mobile">
    1. Open **Dashboard → Boiler Manuals**.
    2. Search for a **brand**, or scroll the brand list.
    3. Tap the brand to see its manuals.
    4. Search by model name, then tap a manual to open the PDF.
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Desktop">
    1. Open **Boiler Manuals** in the sidebar.
    2. Search for a **brand**, or select it from the list.
    3. Search the brand's manuals by model.
    4. Select a manual to open it.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

Manuals you upload yourself are marked **Your company manual**. They are
available to everyone in your company.

## Upload a manual

You can add a manufacturer manual that is not already in the library.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Mobile">
    1. Open **Dashboard → Boiler Manuals** and tap the upload button.
    2. Choose the **Brand**.
    3. Enter a **Manual name**, for example `Greenstar 8000 Life`.
    4. Choose a **PDF manual**. PDF only, up to 50 MB.
    5. Tap **Upload manual**.
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Desktop">
    1. Open **Boiler Manuals** and select **Upload manual**.
    2. Choose the **Brand** and enter a **Manual name**.
    3. Choose the **PDF manual**. PDF only, up to 50 MB.
    4. Select **Upload manual**.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Warning>
  Only upload manuals you are allowed to distribute. Uploaded manuals are
  visible to your whole company.
</Warning>

## Delete a manual

Open the brand's manual list and use the delete control next to the manual. On
desktop you can also delete from the manual's page. Deleting a manual removes it
for everyone in your company, so only remove manuals your team uploaded by
mistake.
