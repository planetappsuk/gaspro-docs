Customers are the people or businesses you work for. Keep a customer's contact
details and address here, then link them to jobs, certificates and invoices.

<Tip>
  A customer is the person or company. The property you visit is a
  [job address](/job-addresses). One customer can have several job addresses.
</Tip>

## Add a customer

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Mobile">
    1. Open **Dashboard → Customers**.
    2. Tap **Create New Customer**.
    3. Enter at least a **Name** and **Email**. You can also add **Mobile**,
       **Home Phone** and **Company**.
    4. Tap **Search for Address** to find the address, or enter the **Apt**,
       **Street**, **City**, **Postcode** and **Country** yourself.
    5. Optionally add a **Next Inspection Date** and **Additional Notes**.
    6. Tap **Save / Continue**.
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Desktop">
    1. Open **Customers** in the sidebar.
    2. Select **Create Customer**.
    3. Enter the customer's **Full Name** and **Email**, plus **Company**,
       **Mobile Number** and **Home Phone** if you have them.
    4. Search for the address or enter **Apartment/Unit**, **Street**, **City**,
       **Postcode** and **Country**.
    5. Optionally add a **Next Inspection Date** and **Notes**.
    6. Select **Create Customer**.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

The **Next Inspection Date** is only used until the customer has a certificate
in GasPro. After that, reminders follow the next inspection date on the latest
certificate. By default GasPro sends a reminder 30 days before the due date; you
can change this in **Settings → Form Settings**.

## Find and view a customer

On mobile, use the search bar at the top of the customer list. On desktop, use
the search box to search by name, email, company or address.

Open a customer to see their record. Use the **Call**, **Text**, **Email** and
**Map** buttons to contact them or open their address.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Mobile">
    The customer page has tabs for **Details**, **Appointments**,
    **Certificates**, **Invoices**, **Quotes** and **Estimates**. When you edit
    a customer, use the **Details** and **Documents** tabs; **Documents** is
    split into **Certificates**, **Invoices**, **Quotes** and **Estimates**.
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Desktop">
    The customer page has tabs for **Edit**, **Appointments**,
    **Certificates**, **Job Sheets**, **Invoices**, **Quotes** and
    **Estimates**. Use the tabs to review the customer's history without
    leaving their record.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

Use **Edit** to update the record, or **Delete Customer** to remove it.

<Warning>
  Deleting a customer is permanent. Check their linked jobs, certificates and
  invoices before you remove them.
</Warning>

## Import customers

Rather than typing each customer, you can import a list:

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Mobile">
    Open **Dashboard → Import → Import Customers**. You can import from your
    phone's address book or from a CSV file. Download the CSV template first so
    the columns match. See [Importing data](/importing).
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Desktop">
    On the **Customers** page select **Import customers**, download the CSV
    template, fill it in and upload it. CSV files can be up to 10 MB. See
    [Importing data](/importing).
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Customer fields

| Field | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| Name / Full Name | The customer's name. Required. |
| Email | Used for sending certificates and invoices. Required for new customers. |
| Company | The customer's business, if they have one. |
| Mobile / Home Phone | Contact numbers shown on the customer record. |
| Apartment/Unit, Street, City, Postcode, Country | The customer's address. |
| Next Inspection Date | Default reminder date until a certificate sets its own. |
| Notes | Anything your team needs to know. |
