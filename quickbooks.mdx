---
title: "QuickBooks"
description: "Connect GasPro to QuickBooks from the mobile app."
---

The QuickBooks integration lets you connect your QuickBooks account so invoices
and customers can be kept in sync.

<Note>
  QuickBooks is currently connected from the mobile app under
  **Settings → Integrations**. The desktop app does not have a QuickBooks
  settings screen.
</Note>

## Connect QuickBooks

1. Open the mobile app and go to **Settings → Integrations**.
2. Find **QuickBooks** and check that the status is **Not Connected**.
3. Tap **Connect**.
4. Sign in to QuickBooks and authorise GasPro.
5. Return to GasPro. The status changes to **Connected**.

## Disconnect QuickBooks

1. Open **Settings → Integrations**.
2. Tap **Disconnect** next to QuickBooks.
3. Confirm the disconnection.

Disconnecting stops future syncing. Records already in QuickBooks are not
deleted.

## If the connection fails

- Make sure you signed in to the correct QuickBooks company.
- Complete the authorisation in the browser window before returning to GasPro.
- Try disconnecting and connecting again.
- If it still fails, contact support.
