---
title: "Subscription and billing"
description: "Manage your GasPro plan, payment method and billing history."
---

GasPro is billed per account, with an additional charge for each extra team
member.

## Plans

| Plan | Price |
| --- | --- |
| Premium Monthly | £7.99 per month |
| Premium Annually | £79.99 per year (+2 free months) |

Choose the annual plan for the best value. Prices are shown in the app before
you confirm.

## Start a subscription

When your trial ends, or if you choose to upgrade, GasPro shows **Choose Your
Plan**. Select **Premium Annually** or **Premium Monthly** and complete checkout.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Mobile">
    Open **Settings**, then follow the subscription prompt or open
    **Subscription → Manage Subscription**.
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Desktop">
    Open **Settings → Subscription** and select the plan you want.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

After payment, your subscription is active immediately.

## Manage an active subscription

When your subscription is active:

- **Manage Subscription** opens the billing portal.
- **Update Payment Method** appears if a payment has failed.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Mobile">
    Open **Settings → Subscription → Manage Subscription**. Use **View Billing
    History** to see past invoices and download PDFs.
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Desktop">
    Open **Settings → Subscription**, then select **Manage Subscription** to
    update your payment method, view invoices or cancel.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Team member charges

Each additional team member is charged at the per-member rate shown when you add
them. The first payment is prorated for the time left in your billing cycle.
Removing a member adjusts future billing.

<Note>
  The exact per-member price is shown in the app and depends on whether your
  account is on monthly or annual billing.
</Note>

## If your subscription expires

If a payment fails or your trial ends, GasPro locks the features that need an
active subscription and shows an upgrade screen. Select **Update Payment
Method** if the card needs changing, or **Upgrade Now** to choose a plan.

Your data is not deleted when a subscription expires. Reactivate the
subscription to regain access.

## Cancel

Use the billing portal to cancel. You keep access until the end of the period
you have paid for. You can reactivate later from the same place.

<Warning>
  Cancelling does not delete your customers, certificates or invoices. Download
  any records you need before your access ends.
</Warning>
