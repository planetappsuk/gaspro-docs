The gas rate calculator and installation volume calculator are available in the
mobile app under **Dashboard → Tools**. Use them on site to check appliance
performance and tightness test results.

## Gas rate calculator

Open **Dashboard → Tools → Gas Rate Calculator**.

1. Choose the gas type: **Natural Gas** or **LPG**.
2. Choose **Metric (m³)** or **Imperial (ft³)**.
3. For metric, choose a test period: **30s**, **60s**, **120s** or **180s**.
4. Start the timer.
5. For metric, enter the **Initial Reading (m³)** and **Final Reading (m³)**.
   For imperial, stop the timer after one dial revolution.
6. Tap **Calculate**.

The result shows:

- **Gas Rate** in m³/hr.
- **Gross** heat input in kW.
- **Net** heat input in kW.

Tap **Reset** to clear the calculator. Tap **Save Calculation** and give it a
name to keep the result. Saved results are in the **Saved Calculations** tab,
where you can review or delete them.

## Installation volume calculator

Open **Dashboard → Tools → Installation Volume & Tightness Test**.

### Test setup

- **Gas type** — Natural Gas, LPG or LPG/air.
- **Test type** — **Pipework only** for new installations, or **Existing** when
  existing appliances remain connected.
- **Display volume in** — m³ or litres.
- **Meter** — U6/G4, E6, U16 or none.

### Pipework

Enter the length in metres for each pipe size you used. Leave unused sizes
blank. Copper and steel sizes are listed separately.

### Tightness test

Enter the **Initial test pressure (mbar)** and the **Observed drop (mbar)**.
Allow one minute for stabilisation, then observe for two minutes. Enter the drop
to two decimal places.

### Result

The result shows the installation volume, purge volume, a volume breakdown and a
verdict:

- **Tightness test PASS**
- **Within permissible limits — retest pipework only**
- **Tightness test FAIL**
- **IV > 35 L · outside UP/1B scope**

Save the calculation with a name to keep it in **Saved Calculations**.

<Warning>
  The calculators are aids. Always follow the current standards and your own
  training when interpreting results.
</Warning>
