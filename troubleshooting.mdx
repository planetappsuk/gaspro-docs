---
title: "Troubleshooting"
description: "Fix the most common issues with sign-in, sync, sending and billing."
---

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I cannot sign in">
    - Check the email address and password. Passwords are case-sensitive.
    - Use **Forgot password?** on the sign-in screen and follow the email link.
    - If you have just been invited to a team, make sure you are signing in with
      the email address the invite was sent to.

    If you still cannot sign in, contact support.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My verification code has not arrived">
    - Wait a minute and check your messages again.
    - Use **Resend code** to request a new one.
    - The code expires after 10 minutes.
    - Check that the phone number is correct and can receive SMS.

    If the number is wrong, contact support before trying again.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Something is missing or out of date">
    - Check your internet connection.
    - Pull to refresh the list.
    - On mobile, look for **Re-Sync Data** or **Resync** in the empty state or
      list controls.
    - Open the record again from the dashboard so GasPro fetches the latest
      version.

    If a record was created on desktop, allow a moment for it to appear on
    mobile.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="An email was not delivered">
    1. Open the certificate or bill and check the email status.
    2. If it shows **Bounced**, check the customer's email address for typos.
    3. Update the customer's email, then send again.
    4. If attachments were added, check the total size is within your email
       provider's limit.
    5. Check the customer's spam or junk folder.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The PDF will not load">
    - Make sure the certificate or bill has finished saving.
    - Check you have an internet connection.
    - Refresh the page or reopen the record.
    - On mobile, reopen the certificate from the certificate list.

    If the PDF still does not load, contact support with the certificate or
    bill reference.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I cannot see another team member's records">
    A team member only sees their own records unless the main user grants
    permission. Ask the main user to open **Manage Users → [member] →
    Permissions** and enable the relevant **View All** permission.

    The same applies to editing other people's calendar events: enable **Edit
    All Calendar Events**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I cannot delete a certificate or bill">
    Deleting certificates, invoices, quotes and estimates is controlled by the
    **Delete Certificates, Invoices, Quotes & Estimates** permission. Ask the
    main account user to open **Manage Users → [member] → Permissions** and turn
    it on.

    If the permission is off, GasPro hides the delete option and blocks the
    request, even if you can open the document.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I cannot add a team member">
    - Only the main account user can manage users.
    - The account needs an active subscription.
    - Adding a member adds a prorated charge to the subscription. Check the
      price shown before saving.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My subscription expired or a payment failed">
    Select **Update Payment Method** on the upgrade screen, or open
    **Settings → Subscription** and manage the subscription. Update the card in
    the billing portal. Access returns as soon as the payment succeeds.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A CSV import failed">
    - Use the GasPro template and keep the column headings unchanged.
    - Save the file as CSV, not XLSX.
    - Fill in the required columns for every row.
    - Try a small batch first to isolate the problem row.
    - On desktop, the file must be 10 MB or smaller.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A boiler manual will not upload">
    - The file must be a PDF.
    - The maximum size is 50 MB.
    - Choose a brand and enter a manual name before uploading.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="QuickBooks will not connect">
    - Complete the QuickBooks sign-in and authorisation in the browser before
      returning to GasPro.
    - Make sure you are authorising the correct QuickBooks company.
    - Disconnect and try again.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Contact support

If none of these solve the problem, visit the
[GasPro support page](https://www.gasproapp.co.uk/contact). Include:

- The email address on your account.
- What you were trying to do.
- Any reference shown on the record (certificate, invoice, job or customer).
- A screenshot or short description of the error, if you have one.
