Settings control what appears on your documents and how GasPro behaves. Most
settings apply to the whole company.

## Mobile settings

Open the **Settings** tab.

| Section | What it controls |
| --- | --- |
| Profile information | Your name, email, phone, Gas Safe ID, oil registration and CSC ID, plus your signature. |
| Business information | Company name, contact details, registrations, bank details, payment terms and logo. |
| Certificate, Invoice, Quote & Estimate Settings | Start numbers, due dates and reminders. |
| Manage Users | Team members and their permissions. |
| GasPro AI | Ask GasPro AI a question, optionally with a photo. |
| Help Centre | Read the GasPro how-to guides. |
| Shoot us a message | Contact the GasPro team. |
| Turn on Notifications | Enable push notifications. |
| Integrations | Connect QuickBooks. |
| Logout | Sign out of the app. |

## Desktop settings

Open **Settings** in the sidebar. The sections are:

- **Profile Information**
- **Company Information**
- **Certificates / Bill Information**
- **Subscription**
- **Update Password**

## Profile

Your profile appears on documents you sign.

- **Name**, **Email** and **Phone Number** are required.
- **Gas Safe ID**, **Oil Registration ID** and **CSC ID** are added where you
  hold them.
- **Signature** — draw your signature to use it on documents. Use **Clear
  Signature** to replace it.

On desktop, saving profile changes may ask you to confirm your password.

## Company

Company details appear on certificates, invoices, quotes and estimates.

| Field group | Fields |
| --- | --- |
| Company | Company Name, Company Email, Phone Number, VAT Number, Industry |
| CC emails | Certificates CC Email, Invoices CC Email, Quotes CC Email, Estimates CC Email |
| Address | Apartment/Suite, Street Address, City, Postcode, Country |
| Registrations | Gas Safe Registration Number, Legionella Registration Number, Oil Registration Number |
| Banking | Bank Name, Name on Account, Account Number, Sort Code, Payment Terms — shown on invoices |
| Logo | Company Logo, used on your documents |

The CC email fields automatically copy your office or accounts team when a
document is sent.

## Certificate, invoice and quote settings

These settings control numbering, due dates and automatic reminders.

### Numbering

- **Invoice Start Number**
- **Quote Start Number**
- **Estimate Start Number**
- **Gas Certificate Start Number**

GasPro uses the next number when you create a new document. Change a start
number carefully if you need your numbering to continue from another system.

### Due dates

- **Default Due Date (Days)** — how many days ahead a new invoice, quote or
  estimate is due.

### Reminders

For certificates, invoices, quotes and estimates you can enable:

- **Email Reminders**
- **SMS Reminders**
- **Days Before Due Date** for certificate service reminders
- **Send a reminder X Days After Invoice Sent** for invoices, quotes and
  estimates

Reminders are sent to the customer using the contact details on their record.
Make sure those details are up to date.

## Notifications

In the mobile app, open **Settings → Turn on Notifications** and allow
notifications when your phone asks. GasPro uses them for appointment reminders
and important updates. If you change your mind, turn notifications off in your
phone's settings for GasPro.

## Delete your account

Account deletion is handled from the website. Open the
[Request Account Deletion](https://www.gasproapp.co.uk/delete-account) page,
complete the form and type `DELETE` to confirm. The GasPro team processes
requests within 48 hours.

<Warning>
  Download any certificates, invoices or reports you need before requesting
  deletion. Account deletion cannot be undone.
</Warning>

## Change your password

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Mobile">
    Open **Settings → Profile information → Change Password**, enter your
    **Current Password**, **New Password** and **Confirm New Password**, then
    select **Update Password**. Passwords must be at least 8 characters.
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Desktop">
    Open **Settings → Update Password**, enter your **Current Password**, **New
    Password** and **Confirm New Password**, then select **Update Password**.
    Passwords must be at least 8 characters.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>
