---
title: "Creating a certificate"
description: "Start a certificate, complete its sections, and finish it with a signature."
---

GasPro has over 20 gas, oil and plumbing records. They all follow the same
basic pattern: choose the type, pick the customer and job address, complete the
sections, then sign and save.

<Tip>
  New to GasPro? Follow the
  [homeowner certificate walkthrough](/certificates/homeowner-walkthrough) for a
  complete example, including adding a customer and job address.
</Tip>

## Step 1: Choose the certificate type

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Mobile">
    1. Open **Dashboard → Certificates**.
    2. Tap the **+** button in the top corner.
    3. Choose a certificate from **Domestic Gas Certificates**,
       **Non-Domestic Gas Certificates**, **Domestic Oil Records** or
       **Other**. See [Certificate types](/certificates/types).
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Desktop">
    1. Open **Certificates** in the sidebar.
    2. Select **Create New Certificate**.
    3. Choose the certificate from the list. See
       [Certificate types](/certificates/types).
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Step 2: Select the customer and job address

Every certificate needs a customer and the property it relates to.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Mobile">
    Select an existing customer or create a new one, then select or create the
    job address. GasPro creates a draft certificate before you enter these
    details, so you can leave and come back to it.
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Desktop">
    Search the address book or create a new customer, then select or create the
    job address. Select **Continue** to open the certificate.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Step 3: Complete the sections

Each certificate has its own sections. Most have:

- A details section with the site, engineer, reference and dates.
- One or more part sections for the checks specific to that record.
- An **Appliances** section where you add each appliance and its readings.
- A **Final Checks** section.
- A **Signature & Completion** section.

Use the **Continue** button, or the tabs/steps shown, to move through the form.
Your entries are saved as you go, so you can return to a draft certificate
later.

### Adding appliances

Open the **Appliances** section and add each appliance. The usual fields are:

- **Type**, **Make**, **Model**, **Location** and **Flue Type**.
- **GCN** and **Serial** — use **N/A** where the label does not have one.
- Combustion readings such as **Ratio**, **CO (PPM)** and **CO2 (%)**.
- Safety checks such as **Operating Pressure**, **Heat Input**, **Safety
  Devices**, **Ventilation**, **Flue and Termination** and **Flue Performance**.

On mobile you can also select an appliance already saved at the job address,
so you do not need to type it again. Tick **Save to Job Address Appliances** to
save a new appliance for future certificates. On desktop, use **Previous
Appliances** to copy an appliance saved at the job address, or the same
**Save to Job Address Appliances** checkbox when adding a new one.

## Photos and attachments

You can attach photos to a certificate from its signature and completion
section:

- **Mobile** — take a photo or choose one from your library.
- **Desktop** — upload one or more images.

Attached images are printed on the certificate PDF. Remove a photo before
sending if it is not needed.

## Find a certificate

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Mobile">
    Open **Dashboard → Certificates**. Use the **All**, **Pending** and
    **Completed** tabs, the search bar, and **Filters** to sort or choose a form
    type. Each row shows the form type, status and email status.
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Desktop">
    Open **Certificates** in the sidebar. Search by certificate, reference,
    customer or location, then use **Filters** for **Certificate Type**,
    **Status**, **Engineer**, **Issued Date** and **Next Inspection Date**.

    Select certificates with the row checkboxes, then choose **Download N
    certificates** to download a ZIP file containing the selected PDFs.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Step 4: Signature and completion

Finish the certificate with:

- **Issued Date**
- **Next Inspection Date**
- **Engineer**
- **Reference**
- **Name** of the person signing
- **Signature**

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Mobile">
    Open the signature screen, sign with your finger, then tap **Save**. Use
    **Clear** to start again.
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Desktop">
    Draw the signature in the signature box, or select **Clear Signature** to
    redo it.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

Select **Save Form** or **Save** to finish. The certificate is then available
to view, download or send.

## Certificate statuses

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Draft | Started but not finished. |
| Pending | In progress or awaiting completion. |
| Completed | Finished and ready to share. |
| Invoiced | An invoice has been raised from the certificate (mobile). |

You can change the status from the certificate's page. On desktop, open the
certificate and select **Change Status**. On mobile, tap the status button at
the top of the certificate.

<Warning>
  Check a certificate carefully before sending it to a customer. If you need a
  corrected version, **Duplicate** the certificate to start from a copy.
</Warning>
