---
title: "Sending certificates"
description: "Download, print or email a completed certificate, and check its delivery."
---

Once a certificate is completed, open it from the certificate list to view it,
save a PDF or send it to your customer.

## View the PDF

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Mobile">
    Open the certificate. GasPro generates the PDF and shows it on the
    certificate page.
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Desktop">
    Open the certificate. The **Certificate** tab shows the PDF. Use the
    download or print controls to save a copy or print it.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Email a certificate

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Mobile">
    1. Open the certificate and tap **Send**.
    2. Enter the recipient **Email Address**. Use **CC** or **BCC** if you need
       to copy someone.
    3. Check the **Subject** and **Message**. GasPro fills these from your email
       template.
    4. Add any extra attachments if needed.
    5. Tap **Send**.
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Desktop">
    1. Open the certificate and select **Send to Client**.
    2. Add the **To** recipients and any **CC** or **BCC** recipients.
    3. Check the **Subject** and **Message**.
    4. Add attachments if needed. The certificate PDF is included.
    5. Select **Send Email**.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Tip>
  Set default CC and BCC addresses for certificates in **Settings → Company**
  (desktop) or **Settings → Business information** (mobile). CC copies fall back
  to the company email when blank. A BCC is only sent when a BCC address is set.
</Tip>

## Share a certificate

On mobile, tap **Share** to open your phone's share sheet and send the PDF
through another app, such as WhatsApp or Files.

On desktop, use the download or print controls on the certificate PDF.

## Delivery status

GasPro records what happened to each email:

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Sent | The email has been sent. |
| Delivered | The recipient's mail server accepted it. |
| Opened | The recipient opened the email. |
| Clicked | The recipient clicked a link in the email. |
| Bounced | The email could not be delivered. Check the email address. |
| Not sent | No email has been sent yet. |

You can see the email status on the certificate page. In the mobile app, use
**Activity** to see a record of what has happened to the certificate. On
desktop, the certificate page shows the current email status.

## Duplicate, invoice or delete

From a certificate you can also:

- **Duplicate** — create a copy to reuse details for a new certificate.
- **Invoice** or **Create Invoice** — raise an invoice from the certificate.
- **Delete** — remove the certificate. This only appears when your user has the
  **Delete Certificates, Invoices, Quotes & Estimates** permission.

<Warning>
  Deleting a certificate is permanent. Download a copy first if you may need it
  again. If you cannot see the **Delete** option, ask the main account user to
  enable the delete permission in **Manage Users → Permissions**.
</Warning>
