---
title: "Example: homeowner certificate"
description: "A complete walkthrough of a Homeowner / Landlord certificate, including adding a customer and job address."
---

This walkthrough follows a typical job from start to finish. Use it as a
practice run or as a checklist the first few times.

**Example job:** Jane Smith at 12 Oak Lane, Leeds, LS1 4AB, needs a Homeowner /
Landlord certificate for her boiler.

## 1. Start the certificate

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Mobile">
    1. Open **Dashboard → Certificates**.
    2. Tap the **+** button.
    3. Under **Domestic Gas Certificates**, choose **Homeowner / Landlord
       Certificate**.
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Desktop">
    1. Open **Certificates** in the sidebar.
    2. Select **Create New Certificate**.
    3. Under **Domestic Gas Records**, choose **Homeowner / Landlord
       Certificate**.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

GasPro creates a draft certificate straight away. If you need to stop, you can
leave it and come back to it from **Certificates**.

## 2. Add the customer

<Note>
  The **customer** is the person or company you work for. The **job address** is
  the property the certificate applies to. They are separate records, but the
  addresses can be the same — many customers own and live at the property. You
  still need to create or select a job address for the certificate.
</Note>

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Mobile">
    The customer list opens.

    **If the customer already exists**, search for them and tap their record.

    **If this is a new customer:**

    1. Tap **Create New Customer**.
    2. Enter their **Name** and **Email**. These are required.
    3. Add **Mobile**, **Home Phone** and **Company** if you have them.
    4. Tap **Search for Address** to fill in the customer's address, or type it
       manually.
    5. Optionally add a **Next Inspection Date** and **Additional Notes**.
    6. Tap **Save / Continue**.
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Desktop">
    The **Add New Customer** page opens with the customer form on the left and
    your **Address Book** on the right.

    **If the customer already exists**, search the Address Book, select the
    customer and then select **Update Customer** to continue.

    **If this is a new customer:**

    1. Enter their **Full Name** and **Email**. These are required.
    2. Add **Company**, **Mobile Number** and **Home Phone** if you have them.
    3. Search for the address, or enter **Apartment/Unit**, **Street**,
       **City**, **Postcode** and **Country**.
    4. Optionally add **Next Inspection Date** and **Notes**.
    5. Select **Create Customer**.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## 3. Add the job address

The job address is the property where the certificate applies. For Jane, this
is 12 Oak Lane. If she owns and lives at the property, this can be the same
address as her customer record — you still need to select or create the job
address so GasPro links the certificate to the property.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Mobile">
    The job address list opens.

    **If the property already exists**, tap it.

    **If this is the first visit to the property:**

    1. Tap **Create New Job Address**.
    2. Tap **Use Selected Customer Information** to copy Jane's contact details
       into the form.
    3. Enter the property address, or tap **Search for Address**.
    4. Add any **Additional Notes**.
    5. Tap **Save**. The certificate opens automatically.
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Desktop">
    The **Add New Job Address** page opens with the address form on the left and
    your **Address Book** on the right.

    **If the property already exists**, search the Address Book, select it and
    then select **Update Address** to continue.

    **If this is a new property:**

    1. Tick **Use customer information** to copy Jane's contact details.
    2. Enter the property address, or search for it.
    3. Add **Notes** if needed.
    4. Select **Create Address**. The certificate opens automatically.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Warning>
  On desktop, the job address you add during a certificate is saved to that
  certificate. If you edit it later on this screen, the address in your address
  book does not change. To update the saved job address permanently, edit it in
  **Job Addresses**.
</Warning>

## 4. Add the appliance

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Mobile">
    1. On the certificate, tap **Add New Appliance**.
    2. Enter **Type**, **Make**, **Model**, **Location** and **Flue Type**.
    3. Add **GCN** and **Serial**, using **N/A** where the label does not have
       one.
    4. Complete the inspection, readings and safety checks.
    5. Tick **Save to Job Address Appliances** so the appliance is available
       next time.
    6. Tap **Save**.
    7. Add any other appliances, then tap **Next** to open Final Checks.
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Desktop">
    1. Use **Previous Appliances** to copy an appliance already saved at the
       property, or fill in the **Appliance Details** form.
    2. Enter **Type**, **Make**, **Model**, **Location** and **Flue Type**.
    3. Add **GCN** and **Serial**, using **N/A** where the label does not have
       one.
    4. Complete the inspection, readings and safety checks.
    5. Keep **Save to Job Address Appliances** ticked to reuse the appliance.
    6. Select **Add Appliance**.
    7. Add any other appliances, then select **Continue** to open Final Checks.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## 5. Complete the final checks

Answer the final safety checks:

- **CO Alarm(s) Fitted** and **CO Alarm(s) Tested**
- **Emergency Control Accessible**
- **Gas Tightness Satisfactory**
- **Gas Installation Pipework Visual Inspection Satisfactory**
- **Equipotential Bonding**
- Optional smoke alarm and inhibitor checks
- **Comments**

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Mobile">
    Tap **Save**. GasPro opens the signature screen.
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Desktop">
    Select **Save Final Checks**. GasPro opens the signature screen.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## 6. Sign and complete

Enter:

- **Issued Date** — today's date.
- **Next Inspection Date** — usually 12 months from now.
- **Engineer** — the person who carried out the work.
- **Reference** — your own reference, if you use one.
- **Name** — the name of the person signing.
- **Signature** — sign in the box.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Mobile">
    Tap the signature box, sign with your finger and tap **Save** on the
    signature pad. Add photos if needed, then tap **Save** in the top corner to
    open the finished certificate.
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Desktop">
    Draw the signature in the box and use **Clear Signature** if you need to
    start again. Add any images, then select **Save Form**.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

You can now view the PDF. Check it before sending.

## 7. Send it to the customer

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Mobile">
    Tap **Send**, check the customer's **Email Address**, the **Subject** and the
    **Message**, then tap **Send**. Use **Share** if you want to send the PDF
    through another app.
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Desktop">
    Select **Send to Client**, add the **To** and **CC** recipients, check the
    **Subject** and **Message**, then select **Send Email**.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

After sending, check the **Email status** on the certificate. If you need to
raise an invoice, use **Invoice** or **Create Invoice** from the certificate.
