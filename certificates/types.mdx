---
title: "Certificate types"
description: "The full list of gas, oil and plumbing records in GasPro."
---

The certificate picker is grouped into four sections. The same types are
available on mobile and desktop unless noted.

## Domestic Gas Records

| Certificate | Use it for |
| --- | --- |
| Homeowner / Landlord Certificate | The standard homeowner or landlord gas safety record, including the CP12-style checks. |
| Gas Warning Notice | Recording an unsafe situation and the warning given. |
| Gas Service Record | Recording a service on a gas appliance. |
| Breakdown Record | Recording a breakdown visit and its findings. |
| Installation/Commissioning Checklist | Commissioning a new installation. |

## Non-Domestic Records

| Certificate | Use it for |
| --- | --- |
| Gas Safety Record | Non-domestic gas safety checks. |
| Leisure Industry Gas Safety Record | Leisure premises such as pubs, hotels and clubs. |
| LPG Safety Record | LPG installations and appliances. |
| Gas Testing & Purging | Testing and purging non-domestic pipework. |
| Catering Record | Commercial catering appliances. |
| Plant Commissioning / Servicing Record (CP15) | Plant commissioning and servicing. |

## Domestic Oil Records

| Certificate | Use it for |
| --- | --- |
| CD/10 Oil Firing Installation Completion Report | Completing an oil-fired installation. |
| CD/11 Oil Firing Servicing and Commissioning Report | Servicing and commissioning an oil-fired appliance. |
| CD/12 Landlord Oil Installation Check | A landlord check on an oil installation. |
| CD/14 Warning and Advice Notice | Recording a warning or advice notice. |
| TI/133D Oil Tank Risk Assessment | Assessing an oil tank and its siting. |

## Other records

| Certificate | Use it for | Availability |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Legionella Risk Assessment | Assessing legionella risk. | Mobile and desktop |
| Unvented Hot Water Cylinder Record | Recording an unvented cylinder installation or service. | Mobile and desktop |
| Air Source Heat Pump Service | Servicing an air source heat pump. | Mobile and desktop |
| Mobile Catering Vehicle / Trailer Safety Record | Safety checks on mobile catering vehicles and trailers. | Mobile and desktop |
| Powerflushing Checklist | Recording a powerflush. | Mobile and desktop |
| Job Sheet | Recording the work carried out on a visit. | Mobile and desktop |
| TMV Inspection & Maintenance Log | Inspecting and maintaining thermostatic mixing valves. | Mobile only |

<Note>
  The certificate picker on mobile shows a **NEW** badge on recently added
  types. This is only a label and does not change how the certificate works.
</Note>
