GasPro AI is a chat assistant in the mobile app. Use it to ask questions and get
help with your business.

<Note>
  GasPro AI is available in the mobile app under **Settings → GasPro AI**. The
  desktop app does not have a GasPro AI screen.
</Note>

## Use GasPro AI

1. Open the mobile app and go to **Settings → GasPro AI**.
2. Type your question in the message box.
3. Optionally attach a photo:
   - **Pick Image from Library** to choose an existing photo.
   - **Take Photo** to use your camera.
4. Tap **Send**.
5. Read the reply. Continue the conversation if you need more detail.

You can remove an attached image before sending.

<Warning>
  GasPro AI is a helper, not a substitute for the current standards, your
  training or your own judgement. Always check important information before
  relying on it.
</Warning>
