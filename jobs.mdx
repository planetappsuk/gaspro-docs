---
title: "Jobs"
description: "Track a piece of work from first enquiry through to payment."
---

A job groups everything for one piece of work: the customer, the job address,
appointments, certificates and billing documents. Use jobs to see the whole
picture instead of hunting through separate lists.

## Create a job

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Mobile">
    1. Open **Dashboard → Jobs** and tap **Create Job**, or use the **Create**
       tab and choose **Job**.
    2. Enter a **Job Title**. This is required.
    3. Add **Details**, a **Reference**, a **Due Date**, **Assigned To**,
       **Priority** and **Status**.
    4. Tap **Save**.
    5. On the job page, use **Customer Details** and **Job Address** to link the
       customer and property.
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Desktop">
    1. Open **Jobs** in the sidebar and select **Create Job**.
    2. Enter a **Job Title**, plus **Details**, **Reference** and **Due Date &
       Time**.
    3. Choose **Assigned To**, **Priority** and **Status**.
    4. Select the **Customer** and **Job Address**. You can create either one
       without leaving the job using **+ New Customer** and **+ New Address**.
    5. Select **Create Job**.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

| Field | Options or meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Status | Open, In Progress, On Hold, Completed, Cancelled, Invoiced, Paid |
| Priority | Low, Normal, High, Urgent |
| Assigned To | A team member, or unassigned |
| Due Date | When the work is due |
| Reference | Your own job reference, for example `JOB-1042` |

## Find a job

On mobile, use the **All**, **Active**, **Completed** and **Cancelled** tabs,
then use the filter and sort controls. You can sort by newest, oldest, due date,
priority or reference, and filter by due date.

On desktop, search by job, reference, customer or location, then use **Filters**
for **Status**, **Priority**, **Assigned To** and **Due Date**.

## Work inside a job

Open a job to see its **Details** and linked records. Both apps have tabs for:

- **Details** — title, description, status, priority, assigned engineer, due
  date, customer and job address.
- **Certificates** — certificates created for this job. Use **Create
  Certificate** on mobile, or the create button on desktop, to start one.
- **Invoices**, **Quotes** and **Estimates** — billing documents linked to this
  job. On mobile, each tab has a **Create** button; on desktop, use the button
  in the tab header.

On desktop, the **Details** tab also includes **Attachments** where you can
upload photos for the job, and **Appointments** with an **Add Appointment**
button. On mobile, appointments are managed from the job's **JobAppointments**
section, where you can also send the customer a **confirmation** or
**reminder** by email or SMS.

<Note>
  You need a customer and job address linked to a job before you can create a
  certificate or invoice from it on desktop.
</Note>

## Change a job's status

Open the job, edit it, and change **Status**. Use **Invoiced** once you have
raised the invoice, and **Paid** once payment has been received. Use
**Cancelled** when the work will not go ahead.

<Warning>
  Deleting a job removes it from your job list. Certificates and invoices that
  were created from the job are separate records, but check them before
  deleting.
</Warning>
