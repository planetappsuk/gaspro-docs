GasPro is available as a mobile app for working on site and a desktop app for
working in the office. Both use the same account, so your customers, jobs,
certificates and invoices are always in sync.

<Tip>
  Sign up once and use the same email address and password on mobile and
  desktop. There is nothing to install or transfer between them.
</Tip>

## Before you start

You will need:

- A valid email address.
- A mobile number that can receive text messages, for the verification code.
- Your company details, such as your company name and Gas Safe registration
  number. You can add the rest later in **Settings → Company**.

## Create your account

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Mobile">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open the app">
        Open GasPro and tap **Create account**.
      </Step>

      <Step title="Enter your details">
        Enter your **email address**, choose a **password** of at least 8
        characters, and enter your **mobile number**.
      </Step>

      <Step title="Accept the terms">
        Accept the **Terms and Conditions** and **Privacy Policy**, then tap
        **Create account**.
      </Step>

      <Step title="Verify your phone">
        Enter the 6-digit code sent to your mobile number. The code expires
        after 10 minutes.
      </Step>

      <Step title="Finish your profile">
        Enter your **full name** and **company name**, then allow notifications
        so GasPro can remind you about upcoming jobs.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Desktop">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open the web app">
        Go to [app.gasproapp.co.uk](https://app.gasproapp.co.uk) and choose
        **Create account**.
      </Step>

      <Step title="Enter your details">
        Enter your **full name**, **email address**, **password** and **mobile
        number**.
      </Step>

      <Step title="Accept the terms">
        Accept the **Terms and Conditions** and **Privacy Policy**, then select
        **Create account**.
      </Step>

      <Step title="Verify your phone">
        Enter the 6-digit code sent to your mobile number. The code expires
        after 10 minutes, and you can request a new one if it does not arrive.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Warning>
  The phone number is required for account verification. Use a number you can
  access now — you will not be able to finish signing up without the code.
</Warning>

## Sign in

Once your account is confirmed, sign in with your **email address** and
**password**. If you forget your password, choose **Forgot password?** and
follow the link sent to your email.

## Mobile or desktop?

You do not have to choose — most engineers use both.

| Task | Recommended |
| --- | --- |
| Creating certificates on site | Mobile |
| Taking photos of appliances and data plates | Mobile |
| Capturing a customer signature | Mobile |
| Raising invoices, quotes and estimates | Either |
| Managing customers, jobs and your calendar | Desktop |
| Importing customers from a CSV file | Desktop |
| Managing team members and company settings | Desktop |

## What to set up first

1. Add your **company details** in **Settings** — **Company Information** on
   desktop, **Business information** on mobile. These appear on your
   certificates and invoices.
2. Add a **profile signature** in **Settings → Profile Information** if you want
   it on documents.
3. Create your first **customer** and **job address**.
