---
title: "Job addresses"
description: "Store the properties you visit, with their appliances and contact details."
---

A job address is the property where you carry out work. It holds the site
contact details and the appliances at that property, so you can reuse them on
future certificates and appointments.

<Tip>
  Create the [customer](/customers) first, then create a job address for their
  property. If the customer owns and lives at the property, the job address can
  be the same address. Use the same customer for landlords with several
  properties, with a separate job address for each one.
</Tip>

## Add a job address

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Mobile">
    1. Open **Dashboard → Job Addresses**.
    2. Tap **Create New Job Address**.
    3. Choose the customer this property belongs to, or create a new customer.
    4. Check the contact details and enter the property address. Tap **Search
       for Address** to fill the address automatically.
    5. Add **Additional Notes** if needed and tap **Save**.
    6. Open the **Appliances** tab to add appliances at the property.
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Desktop">
    1. Open **Job Addresses** in the sidebar.
    2. Select **Create Job Address**.
    3. Enter the site contact's **Full Name** and optionally **Email**,
       **Mobile Number** and **Home Phone**.
    4. Enter the property address, or search for it.
    5. Add **Notes** and select **Create Job Address**.
    6. Use the **Appliances** tab to add the appliances at this property.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Appliances

Appliances belong to a job address. Adding them once means you can select them
again on future certificates instead of re-entering the details.

The fields are:

| Field | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| Type | The appliance type, for example a boiler or fire. |
| Make | The manufacturer. |
| Model | The model name or number. |
| Location | Where the appliance is in the property. |
| Flue Type | The flue arrangement. |
| GCN | Gas Council Number. Use **N/A** when it is not available. |
| Serial No. | The serial number. Use **N/A** when it is not available. |
| Landlord Appliance | Mark appliances that belong to the landlord. |

On mobile, add an appliance from the job address and tap **Save**. On desktop,
open the job address, go to **Appliances** and add the appliance from there.

<Note>
  On mobile you can also import a list of appliances from a CSV file. Open
  **Dashboard → Import → Import Appliances** and download the template first.
</Note>

## Find and view a job address

Search the job address list by contact name, phone, email or address. Open a
record to see the contact details, address, notes and linked appliances. Use the
**Call**, **Text**, **Email** and **Map** buttons to contact the site or open the
property in maps.

On mobile, edit a job address and use the **Details**, **Documents** and
**Appliances** tabs. **Documents** is split into **Certificates**, **Invoices**,
**Quotes** and **Estimates**.

On desktop, a job address also has tabs for **Certificates**, **Job Sheets**,
**Invoices**, **Quotes** and **Estimates**, so you can review everything that has
happened at the property.

From a job address you can:

- Edit the address or contact details.
- Add, edit or remove appliances.
- View certificates and appointments linked to the property.
- Delete the job address.

<Warning>
  Deleting a job address removes it from your list. Check for linked jobs and
  certificates first.
</Warning>

## Import job addresses

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Mobile">
    Open **Dashboard → Import → Import Job Addresses** and choose phonebook or
    CSV. Download the template first. See [Importing data](/importing).
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Desktop">
    On the **Job Addresses** page select **Import job addresses**, download the
    CSV template, complete it and upload it. CSV files can be up to 10 MB.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>
